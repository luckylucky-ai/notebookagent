## ⭐ Today's Language Takeaway

1. **"While the youngest cohort consistently dominated in terms of time spent, the oldest group recorded the most striking proportional increase."**
   → 通用模板：While [A] consistently dominated in terms of [X], [B] recorded the most striking [proportional/absolute] increase.
   → 用途：Task 1 Overview 段，同时点出两个主要趋势

2. **"The most remarkable shift, however, belonged to those aged 50 and over: starting at a mere 1 hour, [they] sextupled their figure to reach 6 hours by 2023 — by far the largest proportional increase, despite remaining the lowest in absolute terms."**
   → 通用模板：The most remarkable shift belonged to [X]: starting at a mere [数字], [they] [倍数词] their figure to reach [数字] — by far the [largest/smallest], despite remaining the [lowest/highest] in absolute terms.
   → 用途：Task 1 最后一段高亮最极端数据

3. **"Satisficers adopt a threshold-based strategy, selecting the first option that meets their predefined criteria."**
   → 通用模板：[Group] adopt a [adj]-based strategy, [V-ing 结果].
   → 用途：描述某类人群行为模式的简洁句式

4. **"Higher income does not necessarily correlate with greater happiness."**
   → 通用模板：[A] does not necessarily correlate with [B].
   → 用途：反驳"A 必然导致 B"的论点，学术写作高频句式

5. **"Architectural interventions can simultaneously preserve autonomy while guiding individuals toward better outcomes."**
   → 通用模板：[措施] can simultaneously [好处 A] while [好处 B].
   → 用途：政策类 Task 2，展示一个措施的双重好处

### 题目 2
> The bar chart shows the average number of hours per week that people in four age groups spent on social media in 2015 and 2023.

### 评分：Band 6.0（比题目 1 退步）

| Criteria | Score | Key Feedback |
|----------|-------|-------------|

| CC | 6.5 | 4 段结构清晰 |
| LR | 6.5 | sextupled / trajectory / surging 用对了，但模板搬太硬 |
| GRA | 6.0 | 多处词形错误 |

### ❌ 两个致命错误

**错误 1：用 first/second group 代替实际标签**
- ❌ first group, second group
- ✅ **18-24 year-olds / the 25-34 age group / adults aged 35-49 / those aged 50 and over**

**错误 2：数据是小时，不能说 percentage points**
- ❌ an increase of 80 percentage points
- ✅ an increase of **7 hours**（绝对增量）
- ✅ an increase of approximately **64%**（相对增幅）

### Task 1 数据单位规则

| 数据类型 | 正确表达 |
|---------|--------|
| 百分比（%） | rose from 12% to 43%, an increase of 31 **percentage points** |
| 小时/数量 | rose from 1 to 6 **hours**, an increase of 5 **hours** |
| 金额 | rose from $200 to $500, an increase of **$300** |

### 默写错误记录（Band 8 范文默写，9 处差异）

| 你写的 | 原文 | 错误类型 |
|-------|------|---------|
| in term of | in **terms** of | 固定搭配永远复数 |
| over 8-year periods | over **the eight-year period** | 冠词 + 单数 + 拼出数字 |
| younger cohort | younger **cohorts** | 复数 |
| ..., it recorded | **while** ..., it recorded | 缺 while（逗号拼接错误） |
| experienced a trajectory | **followed** a trajectory | follow a trajectory 固定搭配 |
| represents a gain | **representing** a gain | 分词短语需要 -ing |
| The two old groups, which starting from significant lower | The two **older** groups, **while starting** from **significantly** lower | 三处错误 |
| sextuple | **sextupled** | 过去时 |
| in absolute term | in absolute **terms** | 固定复数 |

### Band 8 范文（社交媒体柱状图）

The bar chart illustrates the average number of hours per week that people across four age groups devoted to social media in 2015 and 2023.

Overall, social media usage rose across all age groups over the eight-year period, with younger cohorts consistently dominating in terms of time spent. Notably, while the oldest group began from the lowest base by a considerable margin, it recorded the most striking proportional increase of all four groups.

Among the younger demographics, 18-24 year-olds led throughout, spending 11 hours per week in 2015 and climbing substantially to 18 hours by 2023 — an increase of 7 hours. Those aged 25-34 followed a comparable trajectory, rising from 8 to 14 hours over the same period, representing a gain of 6 hours.

The two older groups, while starting from significantly lower bases, also demonstrated notable growth. Adults aged 35-49 saw their weekly usage more than double, from 4 to 9 hours. The most remarkable shift, however, belonged to those aged 50 and over: starting at a mere 1 hour per week, this group sextupled its figure to reach 6 hours by 2023 — by far the largest proportional increase across the chart, despite remaining the lowest in absolute terms.

*(186 words)*

### Band 8 高分句式

| 句式 | 用途 |
|------|------|
| ...consistently dominating in terms of [X] | 说某组"在某维度一直领先" |
| while [A] began from the lowest base, it recorded the most striking [X] | Overview 对比句 |
| following a comparable trajectory | 说两组趋势相似（比 similar 高级） |
| The most remarkable shift, however, belonged to... | 转折 + 强调某组最特别 |
| despite remaining the lowest in absolute terms | 让步限定，防止过度解读数据 |

### Part C: 关键收获 Top 3

2. **必须用图表原有标签**：不能用 first/second group，要用 18-24 year-olds / the 25-34 cohort 等
3. **in terms of / in absolute terms** 永远复数，和 percentage points 一样

### 📚 知识收获：选择悖论（Paradox of Choice）

| 概念 | 解释 |
|------|------|
| 果酱实验（Iyengar & Lepper 2000） | 24 种果酱 → 3% 购买；6 种果酱 → 30% 购买。选项越多，决策越瘫痪 |
| Maximizers（最大化者） | 穷尽比较所有选项找最优解；客观结果更好但主观满意度更低 |
| Satisficers（满足者） | 找到第一个满足标准的就选；满意度更高 |
| Opportunity cost（机会成本） | 选了 A 就会后悔没选 B/C/D → 选项越多，后悔越多 |
| Choice overload（选择过载） | 不是普遍现象，取决于选项复杂度、决策者专业度、选项可比性 |
| Choice architecture（选择架构） | 选项的呈现方式影响决策（Thaler & Sunstein "nudge" theory） |
| Default effect（默认效应） | 器官捐献 opt-out 国家 >90%，opt-in 国家 <20% |
| 结论 | 选择与满意度是曲线关系（curvilinear）：增到一个点后开始下降 |

#### 记忆技巧
- **cognitive**：cognit（知道）+ -ive → 关于认知的
- **threshold**：thresh（打谷）+ hold → 过了这个点才算进来 → 门槛；注意拼写 th-r-e-s-h-h-o-l-d
- **paradox**：para（相反）+ dox（观点）→ 两个相反观点都成立 → 悖论
- **autonomy**：auto（自己）+ nomy（法则）→ 自己制定规则 → 自主权
- **consensus**：con（共同）+ sens（感觉）→ 大家感觉一样 → 共识；注意拼写 consen**sus**
- **criteria**：单数 criterion，复数 criteria（类似 phenomenon/phenomena）；"the criteria **are**" 不是 "is"
- **facilitate**：facil（容易）+ -itate（使）→ 使变容易 → 促进
- **attribute**：词序固定 attribute **X** to **Y**，不能反过来
