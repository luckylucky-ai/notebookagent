## Speaking Part 3: Skills & Learning (预估 5.5)

### 我的错误总结

#### Q1: Why do people find it difficult to learn new skills as they get older?

| 错误 | 纠正 | 要点 |
|------|------|------|
| our memories and energy will **be worse and worse** | our memory and energy levels tend to **decline** | memory 不可数用单数；decline 更精确 |
| we will spend more time on **family stuffs** | spending more time on **family responsibilities** | stuff 不可数，不能加 s |
| We can learn everything **in** anytime | We can learn anything **at** any time | at any time 固定搭配；everything→anything |

#### Q2: Are practical skills less valued?

| 错误 | 纠正 | 要点 |
|------|------|------|
| practical skills **is** as important | practical skills **are** as important | skills 复数用 are |
| cooking a spread **home-style dishes** | cooking a **spread of** home-style dishes | a spread **of** 固定搭配 |
| to make **an enjoyable memories** | **creating enjoyable memories** | memories 复数前不能加 an |

#### Q3: Should schools focus more on life skills?

| 错误 | 纠正 | 要点 |
|------|------|------|
| school should **permit** students | schools should **allow** students | permit 太正式（法律用语），口语用 allow/let |
| choose their **interesting** skills | explore skills **they're interested in** | interesting = 事物有趣；interested in = 人对事物感兴趣 |
| **incentive** their potential | **nurture/unlock** their potential | incentive 是名词，不能当动词 |

#### Q4: How has technology changed learning?

| 错误 | 纠正 | 要点 |
|------|------|------|
| the ways of learning **had** completely changed | the way we learn **has** completely changed | 到目前为止的变化用现在完成时 has changed |
| **make** people **can** easily **access to** | **enable** people to easily **access** | make+can 不能连用；access 做动词不加 to |
| What really surprised **my** | What really surprised **me** | my = 物主代词；me = 宾格代词 |

### 时态重点复习

```
过去 ─────── AI 出现 ─────── 现在
                ↓                ↓
          has changed ← 从那时到现在的持续影响 (现在完成时 ✅)
          had changed ← "在某个过去时间点之前就已经改变了" (过去完成时 ❌ 这里不需要)
```

### 跨模块弱点

1. **时态** — has changed vs had changed，说"到目前为止的变化"用 has
2. **词性混淆** — incentive(n.) vs incentivise(v.), interesting vs interested, my vs me
3. **展开层次** — Part 3 应选 1-2 个点深入展开，而非 3 个点蜻蜓点水

## Model Answers

### Q1: Why do people find it difficult to learn new skills as they get older?

> Well, I think there are **a few things going on**. The obvious one is that **as we age**, our ability to absorb new information **tends to slow down** — it just **takes longer for things to stick**. But **honestly, I think the bigger issue is time**. Once you've got a full-time job, a family, kids to take care of — **there's just not much bandwidth left** for picking up something new. **That said**, I'm a big believer in the growth mindset — **the idea that** you can learn pretty much anything **if you're willing to put in the time**. It's just that finding that time gets harder.

**发音：** "it just **TAKES** longer | for things to **STICK**." — takes 和 longer 连读; things to stick 中 to 弱读 /tə/

### Q2: Are practical skills less valued than academic ones?

> **Not at all** — I'd actually argue they're **equally important**, just in different contexts. Academic skills open doors **career-wise** — they get you qualifications, they help you **think critically**. But practical skills are what **get you through everyday life**. I mean, **there's something really satisfying about** being able to cook a proper meal for your friends — like, **whipping up** a big spread of home-style dishes and just **watching everyone dig in**. That's not something a university degree **teaches you**. **The problem is**, society tends to put academic qualifications **on a pedestal** and **look down on** trades and practical work, which I think is **a real shame**, because **at the end of the day**, a skilled electrician contributes just as much to society as an accountant.

**高分表达：**
- whip up (a meal) = 快速做出
- dig in = 开吃
- put sth on a pedestal = 过度推崇
- a real shame = 真的很遗憾
- at the end of the day = 归根结底

### Q3: Should schools focus more on life skills?

> Hmm, **that's a tricky one**. I wouldn't say it's an either-or situation — I think schools need to do **both**. But **if I had to pick a side**, I'd say schools **could definitely do more** on the practical side. **The thing is**, most kids leave school knowing how to solve quadratic equations but having **no clue** how to file their taxes or cook a decent meal. And those are the things that **actually matter day to day**. So yeah, I think schools should **at least give students the option** to explore practical skills alongside the academic ones — **let them figure out what they're actually into**.

### Q4: How has technology changed the way people learn new skills?

> Oh, **massively**. I think the biggest shift **has been** in accessibility. Like, **back in the day**, if you wanted to learn, say, graphic design, you'd **pretty much have to** enrol in a course or find a mentor. But **nowadays**, you've got YouTube tutorials, online platforms, and — **this is the really game-changing part** — AI tools like Claude or Gemini that can **essentially act as a personal tutor**. You can ask them to explain something **five different ways** until it finally clicks. They can even **generate practice materials** tailored to your level — presentations, quizzes, you name it. **The flip side**, though, is that **having everything at your fingertips** can make people a bit **lazy** — they skim the surface instead of **really diving deep**. So the technology is **incredible**, but you still need the **discipline** to make the most of it.

**高分表达：**
- back in the day = 以前
- game-changing = 颠覆性的
- it clicks = 突然明白了
- at your fingertips = 触手可及
- the flip side = 另一面/反面
- skim the surface = 浅尝辄止
- you name it = 你能想到的都有
